heading, Checkbox, level 2
paragraph
Three states, not two. The parent below is "partially checked" whenever some but not all of its children are -- a reader says so, and a stylesheet can see it on the data-hozo-state attribute without being told twice.
end of paragraph

heading, Switch, level 2
paragraph
Two states, because a thing is on or off and there is no third way to be on. ARIA gives the switch role no mixed value, and a reader says "on" and "off" for it rather than "checked".
end of paragraph
