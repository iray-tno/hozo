heading, A slider and an accordion, level 2
paragraph
The thumb is 24 by 24, which WCAG 2.5.8 asks for and a slider is the control that most invites a 12px dot; the rail is 6px and drawn behind it, so the part a finger has to hit is the whole row. Drag it, and then move it with an arrow key -- it announces "40 percent" rather than "40", because the number is not the answer to how loud.
