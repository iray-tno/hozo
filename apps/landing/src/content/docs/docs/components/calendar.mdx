`Calendar` is a month grid that stays on the page. Click a day, or Tab into the
grid and move with the arrow keys:

<UiExample source={source} file="CalendarDemo.tsx">
  <CalendarDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **Dates are plain records:** `{ year, month, day }`, with `month` from 1 to
  12. There is no `Date` and no time zone, so the 24th stays the 24th
  wherever the page is read.
- **`value`** and **`onChange`** hold the chosen day. With **`range`**, they
  hold `{ start, end }` instead.
- **The keyboard:**
  - the arrows move by a day or a week
  - Home and End go to the start and end of the week
  - Page Up and Page Down move by a month
  - Enter chooses the day
- **`min`** and **`max`** disable the days outside them. That is why the days
  before the 24th cannot be chosen here.
- **`locale`** names the month and the weekdays, and **`firstDayOfWeek`**
  overrides where the week starts.
- **`today`** marks a day. It defaults to the device's date, and the example
  pins it so the page reads the same on any day.
