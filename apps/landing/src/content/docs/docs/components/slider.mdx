A `Slider` is `role="slider"` with the keys the ARIA Authoring Practices list:
the arrows step, Page Up and Page Down take big steps, and Home and End go to
the ends. A drag keeps going past the end of the track. The thumb is 24 by 24,
as WCAG 2.5.8 asks, and the whole row is the target.

- **`value`** and **`onValueChange`**, or **`defaultValue`**.
  **`onValueCommit`** is called once, when a drag or a key press ends.
- **`min`**, **`max`**, **`step`** and **`bigStep`:** 0 to 100 in steps of
  1 by default.
- **`valueText`:** what a screen reader says for a value. Above, it says
  "40 percent" rather than "40".
- **`accessibilityLabel`**, **`disabled`** and **`orientation`**. `className`
  reaches the track, for a width or a margin.
