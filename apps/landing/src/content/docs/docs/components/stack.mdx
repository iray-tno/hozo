`Stack` lays its children out in a row or a column, with a gap between them.
It is how the other examples on these pages space their buttons:

<UiExample source={source} file="StackDemo.tsx">
  <StackDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **`direction`** is `column` by default, or `row`.
- **`gap`** is one of four: `none`, `tight` (8px), `normal` (16px, the default)
  and `loose` (24px). A short list keeps spacing consistent across a screen.
  For any other gap, use a `View` with a `gap-*` class.
- **`align`** places the children across the direction: `start`, `center`,
  `end` or `stretch`.
- **`className`** is added after the stack's own classes.
