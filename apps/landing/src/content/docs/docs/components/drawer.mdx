`Drawer` slides a panel in from the side of the screen, over a scrim. Open it,
then press Escape, click the scrim, or press Close:

<UiExample source={source} file="DrawerDemo.tsx">
  <DrawerDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **`open`** drives it, and **`onClose`** is called when it asks to go away:
  Escape or the scrim. Keep `open` in your state, as with `Dialog`.
- **It is modal.** Focus stays inside while it is open, the page behind it
  stops scrolling, and focus goes back to the opener when it closes.
- **`side`** is `left` by default. `start` and `end` follow the reading
  direction, so `start` is the right-hand side in a right-to-left interface.
- **`accessibilityLabel`** names it, or **`accessibilityLabelledBy`** points at
  a heading inside it.
- **There is no close button**, for the reason `Dialog` has none: put one in
  the children, as the example does.
- **On React Native** it is drawn over a `Modal`, and a swipe toward the edge
  closes it.
