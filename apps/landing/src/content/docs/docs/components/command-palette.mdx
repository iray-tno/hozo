`CommandPalette` opens a search field over the page with a list of commands
under it. Open it, type "dark", then press Enter:

<UiExample source={source} file="CommandPaletteDemo.tsx">
  <CommandPaletteDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **`commands`:** `{ id, label, onSelect, group?, keywords?, shortcut?,
  disabled? }`. Commands with the same `group` are listed under one heading.
  `keywords` match as well as the label, which is why "colour" finds "Toggle
  dark mode".
- **`open`** and **`onOpenChange`** control it. **Opening it is yours to
  wire**, from a button as here or from a key such as Ctrl K in your app.
- **It is a modal dialog holding a combobox.** Focus goes into the field, Tab
  cannot leave the dialog, and Escape or a press outside closes it and puts
  focus back where it was.
- **The arrow keys move through the commands** while focus stays in the field,
  and Enter runs the highlighted one and closes. As the list narrows, how many
  commands match is announced politely.
- **`shortcut`** is shown beside a command. It is a label, and it does not bind
  the key.
