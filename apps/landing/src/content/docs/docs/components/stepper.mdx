`Stepper` shows the steps of a process and which one a person is on. Press Next
and Back, or a step itself:

<UiExample source={source} file="StepperDemo.tsx">
  <StepperDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **`steps`:** `{ label, description?, status? }` for each step.
- **`activeStep`** is the index of the current step. The steps before it are
  completed and the ones after are upcoming, unless a step's `status` says
  otherwise, such as `error`.
- **The status is said in words, not only in colour.** It is an ordered list,
  the current step is `aria-current="step"`, and a reader hears "Step 2 of 4:
  Address, current". The number or check mark is hidden, because the words
  already say it.
- **`onStepPress`** makes the steps buttons. Without it they are text, which
  suits a process that has to be done in order.
