A `SegmentedControl` is a [radio group](../radio-group/) drawn as a strip,
with the same props and the same keys. The chosen segment is `aria-checked`.

It looks like a row of tabs, but it means something different: tabs choose
which panel is shown, and a segmented control chooses a value. Use `Tabs` when
something below changes, and this when a field is being filled in.

<UiExample source={source} file="SegmentedControlDemo.tsx">

</UiExample>

- **`options`**, **`value`**, **`onValueChange`**, **`defaultValue`** and
  **`accessibilityLabel`** are the radio group's.
- The strip is as wide as its segments, not as wide as the form.
