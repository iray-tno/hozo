`Popover` opens a panel next to its trigger button. The panel holds whatever
you put in it, which is why it is a `role="dialog"` and not a menu: a reader
told "menu" expects a list of commands. Open it, then press Escape, click
outside it, or Tab out of it:

<UiExample source={source} file="PopoverDemo.tsx">
  <PopoverDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **`trigger`** is what the button says. The component draws the button itself
  and wires `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` to the panel.
- **`accessibilityLabel`** names the panel, or **`accessibilityLabelledBy`**
  points at a heading inside it. A dialog with no name is one a screen reader
  announces and can say nothing about.
- **Not modal by default.** The rest of the page stays reachable, and the panel
  closes when focus leaves it, so it is never left open behind the page. Pass
  **`modal`** when the contents need the keyboard to stay inside, as a date grid
  does: it then traps Tab and announces `aria-modal` together.
- **`open`** and **`onOpenChange`** control it, or **`defaultOpen`** opens it on
  mount and leaves it uncontrolled.
- **`placement`** and **`offset`** say where the panel goes. It flips to the
  other side when there is no room.
