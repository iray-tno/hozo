`Pagination` shows numbered pages, with previous and next. Press a page, or the
arrows at either end:

<UiExample source={source} file="PaginationDemo.tsx">
  <PaginationDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **`page`** and **`onPageChange`** control it, or **`defaultPage`** leaves it
  uncontrolled. **`pageCount`** is the number of pages.
- **It is a navigation landmark** named "Pagination", holding a list, so a
  reader can jump to it. Each page is named "Page 5", and the current one is
  `aria-current="page"`.
- **Previous and next are disabled at the ends, not removed**, so the controls
  do not move under a pointer.
- **The gaps are ellipses**, hidden from a reader. **`siblingCount`** is how
  many pages show beside the current one, and **`boundaryCount`** how many at
  each end.
- **`getPageHref`** turns every page into a link, which is what a page with its
  own address should be.
