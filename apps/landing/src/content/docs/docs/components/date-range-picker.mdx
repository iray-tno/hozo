`DateRangePicker` works like [Date Picker](../date-picker/), but chooses two
days. Open it, click a first day, then a second:

<UiExample source={source} file="DateRangePickerDemo.tsx">
  <DateRangePickerDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **`value`** and **`onChange`** hold `{ start, end }`. The two days can be
  chosen in either order, and `start` is always the earlier one.
- **The days between are marked** with `aria-selected`, and the two ends are
  styled from data attributes. A reader hears **`rangeStartLabel`** and
  **`rangeEndLabel`** on the ends.
- **`rangeSeparator`** goes between the two dates on the button.
- Everything else is as in Date Picker: the popover is modal, Escape closes it,
  and `min`, `max` and `locale` pass to the calendar.
