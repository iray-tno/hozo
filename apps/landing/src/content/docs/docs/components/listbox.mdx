`Listbox` shows its options as a list that stays on the page. Click an option,
or Tab to the list and use the arrow keys, then Space or Enter:

<UiExample source={source} file="ListboxDemo.tsx">
  <ListboxDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **`options`:** `{ value, label, disabled? }` for each option.
- **`multiple`** lets several be chosen. Then `value` and `onValueChange` hold
  an array, and the list announces `aria-multiselectable`. Without it, they
  hold one value.
- **`value`** and **`onValueChange`** control it, or **`defaultValue`** leaves
  it uncontrolled.
- **The list is one tab stop.** The arrows move between options, and typing a
  letter moves to the next option that starts with it. A disabled option is
  skipped.
- **The chosen options are tinted and bold**, not tinted alone, because colour
  alone fails WCAG 1.4.1. The style comes from `aria-selected`, which is what
  a reader is told.
- **`orientation`** is `vertical` by default. `horizontal` swaps the arrow keys.
- In TypeScript, the option type is inferred from `options` and `value`. When
  it cannot be, as with `multiple`, give it: `<Listbox<string> …>`.
