`Tooltip` shows a short phrase next to the element it wraps. Hover a button,
or Tab to it:

<UiExample source={source} file="TooltipDemo.tsx">
  <TooltipDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **The child is the trigger.** The tooltip adds a ref, the pointer and focus
  handlers, and `aria-describedby` to it, so a screen reader reads the phrase
  after the button's name. The child has to be one element that accepts those
  props, such as a `Button`.
- **`content`** is the phrase. Keep it to one: the bubble stops at `max-w-xs`,
  and anything a reader must know belongs on the page, where touch users can
  see it too.
- **It opens on focus as well as hover**, and Escape closes it. The pointer can
  move from the trigger onto the bubble without it closing.
- **`placement`** is `top` by default, and it flips when there is no room.
  **`openDelay`** and **`closeDelay`** tune the timing.
- **The colours are inverted:** dark on a light page and light on a dark one,
  so it reads as something above the page rather than part of it.
