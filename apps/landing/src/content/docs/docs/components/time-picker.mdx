`TimePicker` is a field with an hour, a minute and, on a twelve-hour clock, a
period. Tab to a part and type, or use the up and down arrows or the buttons:

<UiExample source={source} file="TimePickerDemo.tsx">
  <TimePickerDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **`value`** and **`onChange`** hold `{ hour, minute }`. `hour` is 0 to 23
  even on a twelve-hour clock, which is only a way of writing it.
- **Each part is a spinbutton** with its own name: **`hourFieldLabel`**,
  **`minuteFieldLabel`** and **`periodFieldLabel`**. A reader hears its value,
  and the arrows change it.
- **`step`** is how many minutes the arrows move. It does not refuse a typed
  minute between steps, because rounding what someone typed would lose it.
- **`hour12`** chooses the clock. By default `locale` decides.
- **`min`** and **`max`** bound the time.
