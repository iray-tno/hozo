`BottomSheet` rises from the bottom of the screen. On a wide screen it becomes
a centred panel instead, and it can still be dragged. Open it, then drag the
handle, press Escape, or click the scrim:

<UiExample source={source} file="BottomSheetDemo.tsx">
  <BottomSheetDemo client:visible />
</UiExample>

- **`open`** drives it, and **`onClose`** is called on Escape, on the scrim, and
  when a drag ends low enough.
- **`detents`** are the heights it rests at, as fractions of the sheet: `[1]`
  by default, or `[0.5, 1]` for half, then full. **`defaultDetent`** chooses
  where it opens, and **`dismissBelow`** where a release closes it.
- **With more than one detent the handle is a button**, named by
  **`handleAccessibilityLabel`** ("Resize" by default). Pressing it or using the
  arrow keys changes the size, so nobody has to drag.
- **It is modal**, like `Dialog` and `Drawer`, because a sheet that dims the
  page has already said the page is unavailable.
- **There is no close button**: put one in the children when the design wants
  one visible.
