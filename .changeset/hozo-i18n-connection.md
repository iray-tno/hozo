Add `HozoI18nProvider`, `hozoMessages`, locale/direction hooks and structural adapters for i18next and react-intl. Hozo's component labels, status descriptions and announcements can use the application's existing translation catalog, with English fallback messages. Hozo connects to an i18n library; it does not introduce a second translation engine or require one of these libraries as a dependency.
