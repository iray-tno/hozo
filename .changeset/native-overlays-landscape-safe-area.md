The Native `Drawer`, `BottomSheet` and `Menu` keep clear of a landscape notch or camera cut-out on the left or right. A `Drawer` pads the edge it is against. A `BottomSheet`'s root keeps the sheet off both side insets, and the scrim still covers them. A `Menu` pads both sides of its full-width box.
